## DEMO2024
Title: Synthetic Paper on Trust and Turnout
Abstract: We examine the association between political trust and voter turnout using a cross-sectional survey of 1,200 respondents in a fictional polity. Trust is measured with a 0–10 scale. Results show a positive association (b = 0.12, SE = 0.04). We do not claim causality.
